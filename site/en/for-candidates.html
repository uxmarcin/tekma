Candidate portal

Stage, date, what comes next — on your phone.

After applying, candidates get a link to their own portal. They see which stage they are in, when the next interview is and what to expect. No more “can I ask about my status?”

  • A timeline of stages with dates and interviewers
  • What each interview involves: how long it runs, what we will ask, whether it is recorded
  • Add to calendar in one click

Careers page

One page that shows how you hire.

Tekma builds a careers page from your company profile: values, process, open roles and a promise to reply within 10 days. Custom domain, English version, salary ranges — if you want them.

  • Job posts pulled from the kanban, always up to date
  • Apply without an account, with a CV or from LinkedIn
  • GDPR: consents and retention period recorded with each application
Our promises

Four things every candidate gets

01

A reply within 10 days

Every application gets a reply, rejections included. After 5 days without movement, Tekma reminds the role owner.

Learn more →
02

The same questions

Every candidate for a role answers the same set of questions. Your evaluation rests on what you said.

Learn more →
03

Recording with consent

The notetaker joins only if you agree. Recordings and transcripts are stored in the EU and deleted after the period shown in the portal.

Learn more →
04

Right to an explanation

Ask how AI shaped your evaluation. You get the same reasons the recruiter saw, and a decision made by a person.

Learn more →
Your data

Download, correct or delete your data. In the portal, without writing to anyone.

Your request goes to the company with a 30-day deadline and a countdown on their dashboard. After a rejection, Tekma deletes your data on its own once the set retention period ends, unless you agree to stay in the database.
