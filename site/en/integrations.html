Ask Claude: “Who has been waiting longest for Senior Backend?”

Tekma offers a read-only MCP server. Connect it in Claude and ask about kanbans, candidates and reports in plain language, without opening the dashboard. The AI does not change stages or scores; a person still does that in Tekma.

  • Permissions as in Tekma: you see what you have access to
