After the hire

The offer isn't the end.
It's day 1 of 90.

The new hire gets a plan for the first 90 days, with reviews at days 7, 30, 60 and 90. The manager gets a 1:1 rhythm and a weekly mood check-in. Tekma suggests what to ask and tells you when someone starts to fade.

Check-in

One minute on Friday. Only the manager sees it.

Five questions on a phone: how the week went, what shaped it, what went well, what is blocking. Answers don't go to HR or leadership, only to the direct manager.

  • Mood 1–5 with tags: pace, team, goals, support
  • Check-in streak and trend on the profile
  • A drop in mood shows up on the manager's dashboard
app.tekma.app/ludzie/katarzyna-mazur/1-1
1:1

A shared agenda and a prompt for what to ask.

Both sides add topics. Tekma adds a suggestion based on the latest check-in and goals, for example: “Katarzyna wrote that she is firefighting less. Ask what she would like to take on.”

  • Every 1 or 2 weeks, scheduled from the calendar
  • Only the two of you see the notes
  • Overdue 1:1s show on the dashboard and in Slack
Goals

Company → team → person. Everyone sees what their work contributes to.

Company goals broken down by team and person, with progress and a deadline. From day one, the new hire knows what to deliver in 90 days, because you wrote it down during hiring.

  • 90-day expectations carry over from the job post
  • Progress per goal and per person
  • Reviews at day 30, 60 and 90
app.tekma.app/cele
Training

Short daily refreshers instead of a video nobody finishes.

Internal courses and certifications as 2–3 minute refreshers on a phone. Streaks, progress, a voucher on completion. The manager assigns, the person does it in the coffee line.

  • Book club, on-call onboarding, certifications
  • Course refreshers on mobile
  • Progress visible on the profile
