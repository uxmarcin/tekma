AI in Tekma

AI suggests.
A human always decides.

Tekma uses AI where it saves time and reduces bias: CV matching, score suggestions with a quote, a coach during the interview. It never rejects or moves candidates anywhere, and that can't be switched on.

How AI behaves in Tekma

01

Always with a quote

CV match

A 0–100 score and five reasons why.

AI compares the CV with the role's criteria and shows what fits and what doesn't, as a list, not a black box. The recruiter sees the gaps right away and decides whether to invite.

  • Criteria come from your rubric, not a generic model
  • “Why this score?” in one click
  • Candidates can ask for the same explanation in the portal

Score suggestion

A suggested score for every question — with a passage from the interview.

After the interview, AI sets the candidate's answer against the score descriptions in the rubric and suggests a score. The interviewer accepts, changes or rejects it. They see others' scores only after submitting their own.

  • 1–5 score descriptions from the rubric, the same for everyone
  • A gap of 2+ points between interviewers gets a flag
  • Calibration: who scores more leniently, who more strictly
Live coach

During the interview: what you haven't asked yet.

The notetaker listens and checks against the rubric. When there is no evidence yet for a criterion, say “trade-offs,” it suggests a question. Only interviewers see it, and the candidate has consented to the recording.

  • Live rubric coverage
  • PL/EN transcription with speaker recognition
  • Next questions with timings, so you finish within 45 min

Candidate comparison

Three people, one rubric, one table.

After a stage, Tekma lines up candidates question by question. AI summarizes strengths and risks, and you make the decision looking at the same data.

  • Scores weighted by question weights
  • Disagreements and quotes at hand
  • A “move forward / no” recommendation is only a suggestion
Oversight and log

Every suggestion leaves a trace. Every decision has an owner.

Under Settings → GDPR & AI Act you turn on recording consent, explanations for candidates and a monthly bias report. One setting is always on: AI only suggests.

Questions

Common questions about AI

Which model is behind Tekma?

Models from the Claude family, running in the EU. You can see the model version next to every suggestion in the log.

Does the AI learn from our candidates' data?

No. The data isn't used to train models, ours or the provider's. It's used only to generate suggestions for you.

What does the candidate see?

In the portal they can ask for an explanation of how AI affected their assessment. They get the same reasons the recruiter saw.

Can I turn AI off entirely?

Yes, per role or for the whole company. Rubrics, scorecards and the 90-day plan work without AI.

What about the EU AI Act?

Hiring is a high-risk use case (Annex III). Tekma provides human oversight, a decision log, information for candidates and bias monitoring. You get the documentation in the dashboard.
